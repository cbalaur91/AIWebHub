# aiwebhub.io — Keyword research (seed "web design", US/English, 1 Aug 2026)

Source: DataForSEO API only. Full HTML report delivered in session. Companion to `claude/aiwebhub-io-site-explorer-2026-07-31.md`.

## Two framing findings

1. **The national seed is a dead end.** Keyword Ideas on "web design" (US, vol>20, limit 200) returned 200 rows. Filtered locally for `michigan|ann arbor|detroit|near me|local` → **0 matches**. Top result was "google sites" at 673,000/mo. Nationally the term expands into DIY builders. All usable keywords came from geo- and service-seeded expansions.
2. **There is no transactional tier.** All 30 keywords sent to Search Intent came back commercial / informational / navigational. Zero transactional. Considered-purchase category; the group was left empty rather than padded.

**Never sum variant volumes.** Google Ads returns identical figures for synonyms — "web design michigan" / "michigan web design" / "web design in michigan" each return exactly 480. Six Michigan-company variants each return exactly 320. All cluster figures are lead-keyword volumes.

## Low KD, HARD SERP — do not be fooled

- **website redesign cost** — KD **1** (lowest in dataset). SERP: AI Overview, Reddit, LinkedIn, Clique Studios, Innowise, **Forbes Advisor**.
- **how long does it take to build a website** — KD 10, 590/mo. SERP: AI Overview + PAA table, Reddit, **Wix**, Quora, **Forbes**, Elementor, **Bluehost**.
- **ai integration services** (KD 5) / **ai agent development company** (KD 4) — **IBM #1** and **N-iX #1** respectively, plus LeewayHertz, Appinventiv, Master of Code. The two lowest KDs sit on two of the hardest SERPs.
